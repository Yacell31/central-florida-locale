# Asset provenance

All architectural photography currently served by the site is AI-generated illustrative imagery produced for this project. It does not depict an active listing, a verified view of a named community, or Yacell. The user-supplied concept images informed design only; their portraits, claims, statistics and photographs were not copied as business facts.

- `public/images/home.png` and `home.jpg`: illustrative Florida-style house at dusk.
- `public/images/interior.png`, `interior.jpg`, `living.jpg`, `detail.jpg`: illustrative residential interior and optimized variants/crops.
- `public/images/community-1.jpg` through `community-4.jpg`: separate illustrative Florida residential/lakeside/street scenes extracted from a generated four-scene image.
- `src/app/fonts/`: DM Sans and DM Serif Display from their Fontsource npm packages, distributed under the SIL Open Font License. See `public/licenses/`.
- Resource cover artwork: original HTML/CSS composition.
- Text worksheets: original educational planning materials created for this project.

Generated originals are also retained outside this checkout in `/workspace/generated_images`. Optimized JPGs are used in content cards; the hero currently uses the PNG through Next image optimization. Replace assets through the same paths or update content JSON and metadata together. Obtain and review the rights for replacement photos before publication.

## Brand logo

`public/images/brand-logo.png` is a transparent, web-prepared derivative of the logo supplied in chat, created with the image-editing tool. It replaces the earlier text-built logo in both header and footer. The navy/purple/gold identity is retained; the original source artwork should be used for print production or any workflow requiring exact original pixels.
