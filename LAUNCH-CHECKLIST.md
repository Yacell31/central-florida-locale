# Before public launch

## Owner and brokerage approval

- [ ] Confirm the full professional name, approved biography and licensed role.
- [ ] Supply brokerage name, required advertising language, license details and approved contact information.
- [ ] Review visible copy and compliance disclosures with the appropriate brokerage/legal reviewers.
- [ ] Review privacy, terms and accessibility drafts, retention rules and a verified contact process.
- [ ] Supply an approved portrait; no reference-image portrait is represented as Yacell.
- [ ] Confirm photography rights and replace illustrative community scenes with verified local photography if desired.

## Content

- [ ] Approve the four introductory community research guides.
- [ ] Supply sourced HOA/CDD, school, amenity, geographic and market information before making detailed factual claims.
- [ ] Record source URLs, reporting periods, geography and verification dates.
- [ ] Approve educational articles and assign genuine publication/update dates.
- [ ] Review the seven original text worksheets; consider branded PDF editions.
- [ ] Either complete the investor analyzer or keep it clearly forthcoming.
- [ ] Confirm events, streaming URLs, social profiles and schedule links before publishing them.

## Services

- [ ] Configure and test the HTTPS lead-delivery endpoint, including provider-side persistence and duplicate handling.
- [ ] Verify inquiry receipt and newsletter consent/unsubscribe workflows end to end.
- [ ] Add durable rate limiting or a reviewed anti-abuse provider and any required challenge UI.
- [ ] Configure a real scheduler if instant booking is desired.
- [ ] Obtain approved IDX authorization before adding listings. No listing scraping is implemented.

## Verification and publication

- [ ] Recheck content validation, lint, TypeScript, production build and browser tests after final content/integration changes.
- [ ] Review mobile, tablet and desktop screenshots.
- [ ] Verify schema and social previews against approved identity/content.
- [ ] Perform a final accessibility review and real-device testing.
- [ ] Measure deployed performance. Local checks are not field Core Web Vitals.
- [ ] Deploy only after authorization; configure domain, HTTPS and provider production settings.
- [ ] Enable indexing only after all publication blockers are resolved and rebuild.
