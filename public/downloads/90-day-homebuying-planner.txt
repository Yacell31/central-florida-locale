90-Day Homebuying Project Planner
=================================

Days 1–30: Define and prepare
[ ] Write down why you want to move and your target timeframe.
[ ] Separate must-haves from preferences.
[ ] Meet with a qualified lender and gather requested documentation.
[ ] Build a full ownership budget, not just a purchase-price budget.
[ ] Questions for my team: __________________________________

Days 31–60: Explore and compare
[ ] Research your shortlist of areas and verify property-specific details.
[ ] Tour homes and keep consistent notes.
[ ] Collect insurance, maintenance and association information.
[ ] Revisit priorities as you learn.

Days 61–90: Decide and coordinate
[ ] If you find a suitable property, review proposed terms with your team.
[ ] Use your actual contract to track inspections and due-diligence dates.
[ ] Confirm financing requirements, closing steps and handoff details.
[ ] Adjust this framework to your circumstances; no 90-day outcome is promised.

General educational planning resource. Not legal, tax, lending or financial advice. Verify current details with qualified professionals.
