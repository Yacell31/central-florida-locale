New Construction Checklist
==========================

Before committing
[ ] Understand representation and obtain qualified advice.
[ ] Review builder contract terms, deposits and cancellation provisions.
[ ] Confirm included finishes versus upgrades.
[ ] Ask how pricing, completion dates and change orders are handled.

During construction
[ ] Keep selections and approvals documented.
[ ] Discuss independent inspection opportunities and access.
[ ] Track financing requirements and rate-lock implications.
[ ] Confirm association documents, assessments and utility arrangements.

Before handoff
[ ] Review punch-list items, warranties and service procedures.
[ ] Confirm final inspection and completion documentation.
[ ] Keep contact details and written commitments together.
[ ] This is a question framework, not a substitute for contract or technical review.

General educational planning resource. Not legal, tax, lending or financial advice. Verify current details with qualified professionals.
